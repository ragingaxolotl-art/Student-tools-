03 / Setup guide & experiment ideas
  1. Set actual floor dimensions and trial settings. Connect a webcam; the requested 4K resolution may differ from its supported mode.
  2. Calibrate: click floor corners clockwise from top left. Mark two circles or rectangles as prompted.
  3. Place a ruler flat on the floor; click the endpoints of the known span near each end. Both checks must pass. Remove it afterward.
  4. Mask glare if needed. Remove subjects and hands; keep fixtures in place. Capture the empty reference.
  5. Add subjects. Check each has one marker, even while resting. Adjust detection settings before starting; geometry changes require recalibration.
  6. Mark events and exclude invalid intervals. Finish and download data before closing the tab.

Explore beyond lanternflies

Dish choice: compare approaches with identical-dish controls and balanced treatment sides. Activity: compare movement/resting under stable conditions. Objects: track flat tokens or low toy robots. Blank control: check false detections without subjects.

Measure stationary tokens first to estimate jitter. Use independent trials for replication; tracks and video frames are not independent animals. Proximity does not prove feeding. Wall climbing, flight, height, deep-water swimming and occlusion invalidate floor-plane estimates. This is an experimental prototype, not species recognition.

Visits use an entry threshold, exit margin and minimum duration. Missing detections break IDs. Warnings do not automatically exclude data. Downloads include raw observations, quality records, events, settings and track summaries. Optional video support depends on the browser.
